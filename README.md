### What I build

I work at the intersection of **backend APIs, real-time communication, stateful third-party integrations, and fault-tolerant workflows**. My approach begins with understanding business requirements and failure modes, then defining clear contracts, implementing the system, and testing how it behaves when things go wrong.

I am currently a remote engineer working on U.S. healthcare technology projects. I also **independently engineer WOVO LOVE end to end**, from its mobile application to its backend services, data model, security boundaries, and QA. The WOVO application remains a **private, development-stage product**—not a claimed public launch.

### Selected engineering work

**01 / WOVO LOVE — end-to-end product and messaging engineering**  ·  *Sole engineer; application source private*

Designed and developed the React Native/Expo product and its **TypeScript/Deno, Supabase Edge Functions, and PostgreSQL** backend. Implemented private one-to-one **text, voice, and moderated-photo messaging**, with server-side authorization, idempotent writes, ordered messages, retry-aware delivery, real-time updates, and asynchronous push workflows. Built database-backed safety and account lifecycle operations, including scheduled evidence preservation with integrity checks and development-environment recovery validation.

[Public landing-page repository](https://github.com/sunnykumar-dev-105/wovo-love-landing) · [Technical case notes](./BACKEND_ENGINEERING.md#wovo-love--messaging-and-reliability)

**02 / eClinicalWorks healthcare integration — professional backend engineering**  ·  *Employer source private*

Engineered and deployed **Python/FastAPI patient-search and encounter-history APIs** integrating with a stateful EHR. Worked on authenticated session management, Playwright-assisted integration, transport and response parsing, pagination, defensive matching, fail-closed results, runtime recovery, and containerized deployment. Historical regression checkpoints recorded **3,133 patient-search** and **3,004 encounter-history** passing tests in separate suites, each with approximately **92% reported coverage**; these are **not additive unique-test counts**.

[Technical case notes](./BACKEND_ENGINEERING.md#eclinicalworks--reliable-ehr-apis)

**03 / FHAIDA CRM — real-estate operations (2025)**  ·  *Historical Flutter project*

Developed a real-estate CRM prototype covering leads, customer contacts, properties, task follow-ups, and business-card capture. This refers to the **original CRM project**, not a later financial-integrity or revenue-assurance implementation.

[Historical public source](https://github.com/sunny1921/FHaiDA_CRM) · [Project notes](./PROJECTS.md#fhaida-crm--2025)

**04 / Sustainability, research, and earlier product engineering**

Built Flutter/Firebase point-of-sale workflows and explored ESG resource tracking, BRSR-related reporting, and environmental learning applications. Co-developed research-backed projects recognized by national innovation programs; published an IEEE ICCCNT 2024 paper on sustainability education.
